If you're a Micro.blog user who uses my <strong><a href="https://github.com/jimmitchell/mnml">mnml</a></strong> theme, I made a few updates I've been ruminating on for a while that makes it a little more customizable.

Once you've updated from your Micro.blog plugins page, you'll see a new settings option to manage the customizations.<!--more-->

### mnml Settings

Each of these settings are outlined below...

### The Custom Icon

<img src="/media/2024/mnml-custom-avatar.webp" width="600" height="82" alt="Auto-generated description: A camera icon is displayed next to the text Jim Mitchell.">

Many Micro.blog themes are coded to use your main account profile photo for the header image. If you run multiple blogs on the <a href="https://micro.blog/about/pricing">Premium plan</a> and want to use the same theme for each of them, your sites aren't easily distinguishable.

If you <em>do</em> want to distinguish one site from the other, this setting is how you do it with <strong>mnml</strong>. It also saves you from having to use some weird image for your Micro.blog account in order to brand your site the way you want.

All you have to do is add the <strong>complete URL</strong> to any image, and it will display instead of your profile photo. I recommend a simple image no larger than 256 pixels square for this, but feel free to experiment.

If you leave the URL field empty, <strong>mnml</strong> uses your Micro.blog account profile photo instead.

Easy-peasy.

### Categories on the Archives Page

<img src="/media/2024/mnml-archive-categories.webp" width="600" height="146" alt="Auto-generated description: A menu bar labeled Archives contains tags for Photos, Thoughts, Posts, Videos, Links, mnml, and WeekLog.">

Some people have asked how they can show their blog's categories on the Archives page, while others have told me how much they like the fact <strong>mnml</strong> doesn't do that.

Being the people pleaser I am, I came up with a way to make everyone happy. If you want to show categories, toggle the setting on and like magic, they'll <a href="/archive/">show up</a> just like in the screenshot above. If you don't want to display them, leave the setting off.

### Footer Credits

<img src="/media/2024/mnml-footer-credits.webp" width="600" height="68" alt="Auto-generated description: The text mentions hosting by Micro.blog, powered by mmm, and designed by Jim Mitchell.">
