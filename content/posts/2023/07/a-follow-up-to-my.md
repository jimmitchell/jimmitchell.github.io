A follow up to my AirPod drama… I splurged extra for the AirPods Pro 2. As someone with extreme <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9ni%C3%A8re%27s_disease">Meniere’s Disease</a>, I struggle to hear anything in even mildly noisy situations. I’ve found Transparency mode to be a godsend. I tuned ambient noise off &amp; can finally hear what’s being said to me. 🥹
