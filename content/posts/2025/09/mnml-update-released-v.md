The latest version of **mnml** (v2.7.6) adds the following:

- Polish localization. Thanks to [Sebastian](https://micro.blog/bstn)
- Minor tweaks to the Open Graph image styles
- Javascript to the page redirect for a more robust solution
- General code clean up for overall project consistency (add/remove whitespace)

As always, you can upgrade through your plugins listing at your convenience.

A full list of changes can be [found here](https://github.com/jimmitchell/mnml/compare/2.7.5...2.7.6).

Believe it or not, no styles were changed this time around... 🤯
